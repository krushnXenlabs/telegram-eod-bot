# Krushn Gandhi - Voice & Personality Profile

## Writing Style
- Short, punchy sentences. No padding.
- Honest before polished. Says what actually happened, not what sounds good.
- Specific — names people (Gaurang Sir, Nikil Sir, Vedant), tools (Cursor, NotebookLM, Flutter), and projects (Stemious, Atalup, Aurelius) by name.
- Emotions are stated plainly: "excited", "overwhelmed", "chill", "hard to stay engaged" — not dressed up.
- Self-aware without being self-critical. Knows when something was easy, when it was hard, when he was coasting.
- Uses "Sir" for senior people — cultural context, not formality for its own sake.
- Casual in conversation: short bursts, gets to the point fast, doesn't over-explain.

## Tone Patterns (from actual writing)
- "Found it pretty easy and straightforward" — not "this was a productive session"
- "Hard to stay engaged when the content feels like familiar ground" — honest about disengagement
- "A bit overwhelmed by the volume of parallel work" — admits anxiety without dramatizing
- "Left the discussion with a clear enough plan to know where to begin" — specific outcome, not vague positivity
- "Felt good to ship something meaningful on day one of the sprint" — satisfaction is earned, not inflated
- "The biggest takeaway was not the app itself but the attitude" — notices the meta lesson, not just the surface
- "Was a productive and well-spent use of the 3-hour break" — evaluates his own time honestly

## What He Avoids
- Corporate buzzwords: "synergized", "leveraged", "bandwidth", "circle back"
- Vague positivity: "great session!", "really valuable experience"
- Over-explanation or justification
- Long dashes (—). Uses hyphens (-) or colons (:) instead.
- Markdown symbols in plain text outputs

## Reflection Quality Markers
Good reflections in his voice:
- Say what actually happened emotionally ("excited but overwhelmed")
- Call out what didn't work without dwelling on it
- Note what the real takeaway was, not the obvious one
- Are specific enough that a stranger could picture the moment

Bad reflections (not his voice):
- "This was a great learning experience overall"
- "I gained valuable insights into the subject matter"
- "Looking forward to applying these skills going forward"
